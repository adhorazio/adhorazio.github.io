Mean-field equations
Mean field of an infinite network of all-to-all coupled eQIF neurons whose excitabilities η follow a Lorentzian of centre η̄ and half-width Δη (Eq. 24 of the paper). r is the population firing rate; V, Iw and gsyn are population averages (V̄ on the plots). The reduction assumes Vpeak = −Vreset → ∞; the adjusted current I* puts back the finite Vpeak and Vreset of the network (Gast et al. correction, SI Eq. 42).
