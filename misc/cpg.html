The circuit
Each neuron inhibits the one before it in the ring (N2 ⊣ N1, N3 ⊣ N2, N1 ⊣ N3), so activity is handed forward, N1 → N2 → N3: a three-phase rhythm, like the central pattern generators that drive swimming, walking or chewing. A neuron crawls while it waits near threshold, then whips over the top when it fires. Set the inhibition to 0 and the rhythm falls apart.
