How to see hysteresis
1.At Ieff = 38 µA the system is silent — below Ion = 42 µA without Iw+. Operating point on the lower (blue) branch.
2.Click Trigger pulse — Iw+ pushes Inet above 42 µA. Persistent spiking begins; operating point jumps to the upper (green) branch.
3.Slowly drag Ieff down — spiking persists below Ion, down to Ioff (orange). Then it collapses.
4.Raise Ieff — stays silent until Ion = 42 µA (yellow). Same current, different state: hysteresis.
★Circuit: Ion=42 µA, Iblock=98 µA, I₀=70 µA. f₀=f(70)=1.09/τm. Cm sets the frequency scale. Click Auto-scan to trace the loop.
