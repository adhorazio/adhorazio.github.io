How to see hysteresis
1.At Ieff = 38 mV the system is silent — below threshold without Iw+. The operating point sits on the lower (blue) branch.
2.Click Trigger pulse — Iw+ jumps to the self-sustaining value. Persistent spiking begins; the operating point jumps to the upper (green) branch.
3.Slowly drag Ieff down — spiking persists well below the silent threshold, down to Ioff ≈ 34 mV (orange marker). Then it collapses.
4.Raise Ieff again — the system stays silent until Ion = 42 mV (yellow marker). Same parameter, different state: that is hysteresis.
